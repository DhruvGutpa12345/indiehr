# Where a role of ours may see a canary marker (P1-T11b3; design-b.md "After the attack" §10
# item 18). The world (ihr_access.testing) puts a named marker (testing.marker) in every field
# our roles must not see; test_role_matrix.RoleMatrix.test_clinical_canary_never_reaches_restricted_roles
# fails on any marker in any answer to Receptionist, Cashier, TPA Desk, Owner, MRD (no grant),
# Lab Technician, Store Keeper or Radiologist, except where a line below allows it.
#
# <who> <marker> <route key glob> allow|never  # why
#   who: a role or a group of role_matrix.txt; the glob matches role_matrix.txt's keys ("*")
#   allow: that marker may appear there, for that role; the line must be used by the crawl
#   never: the negative row: that marker never appears there for that role, though a care
#     role sees it on those routes. Every allow line has one (the same role and marker
#     elsewhere, the same role and routes with another copy's marker, or another role on the
#     same routes): test_canary_exemptions_are_narrow.
# "base" is the unnamed CANARY (a patient's extension, a visit's discharge advice).
